How to use the app

Study: flash cards

Pick a deck
ALL, GEN, ENR or AD. Each deck is a part of the AIP.
Tap the card
Read the question and think of your answer, then tap the card (or press Space) to reveal it. Tap again to hide it.
Back / Next
Move through the cards without revealing the answers, like scanning down your strip board.
Again
Not sure yet? The card goes to the back of the pile and comes round again later. "Say again."
Got it
You knew it. The card is marked learned and leaves the pile. "Readback correct."
Learned counter
At the top, e.g. "12 / 110 learned": how many cards in this deck you have marked Got it.
Shuffle deck
Mixes the cards still in your pile into a new order. A new sequence, same traffic.
Reset my progress
Puts every learned card back into the pile and sets the counter to 0. A clean board for a new shift.

Quiz

10 questions
Tap Quiz, pick a deck, then choose one of four answers for each question. On a computer you can press 1 to 4.
Instant check
A right answer turns green with a ✓. A wrong one turns red with a ✗ and the right answer shows in green. Tap Next question to carry on.
Your score
At the end you see your score and your best for that deck. Beat it for a "New best!". Get all 10 right for a perfect score.
No repeats
Each quiz picks questions you have not had yet, until you have seen the whole deck. Then it starts a fresh round.
Pro tip: add to your home screen

Open the app in one tap, like any other app.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Add (choose Create shortcut if asked).

Your progress is saved on this phone or browser only. Spot a wrong answer? After the answer shows, tap "Flag this answer as wrong".
